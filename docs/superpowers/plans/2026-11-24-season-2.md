# Season 2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Split EXP and coins into seasons by play date (SS2 from 2026-11-30) while Level keeps climbing on lifetime EXP. Turn the mission summary into a frozen Season 1 record with an EXP top 5. Add a Season 1 souvenir shelf, a coin detail sheet and the new coin look (style C).

**Architecture:** Nothing is stored. `lib/seasons.ts` defines the season windows. `lib/seasonProgress.ts` filters a player's sign-ups and the club calendar per season and runs the existing `buildPlayerProgress` once per season. Quest EXP is scored per season by clipping each quest's window to the season. Every page (profile, leaderboard, summary, home) reads through the same seasoned function, so they cannot disagree.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16 (read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before touching routing APIs), Prisma 7 + Neon, Tailwind, Vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-season-2-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- SS2 starts `Date.UTC(2026, 10, 30)`. A session belongs to SS2 when `session.date >= that`. Session dates are UTC midnight of the local play day.
- No migration and no schema change. Deploying before 2026-11-30 must leave every number exactly as it is today; only the coin look changes.
- Level = `levelProgress(sum of all seasons' EXP totals)`. Leaderboard order = the current season's EXP.
- The `founding-member` coin exists only in SS1.
- Quest EXP is split at the season boundary: clip each quest's window to the season.
- Copy is Thai. Season labels are "Season 1" and "Season 2". The souvenir shelf title is "🏛️ ที่ระลึก Season 1". Tier labels under coins are `COMMON` / `RARE` / `EPIC` / `LEGEND`.
- Dev uses the live database: read only. Never write test data. Simulate dates with a `now` parameter, not by changing data.
- Do not commit `logo2026.png`, `backfill-athlete-links.mjs` or `.superpowers/`.
- Animations must stop under reduced motion (add new classes to the list in `app/globals.css`).

## Review Focus

1. **A quest that crosses 30 Nov with a single target** (for example "play 10 games"): clipped to each half, it may complete in neither. Pin that a per-day quest pays each half its own days. Task 3 adds a test that shows the single-target behaviour, so the choice is visible.
2. **A player with no SS2 play yet**: the profile must show season EXP 0 and an empty coin grid without crashing, the shelf must show, and the player must be left off the leaderboard (Task 2 test, Task 5 check).
3. **Before 30 Nov**: every page must show today's numbers, no shelf and no "Season 2" label (Task 2 test with `now = 2026-11-29`, Task 8 live read-only check).
4. **The streak across the boundary**: SS2's first day pays no streak bonus even if the player came on 25 Nov as well (Task 2 test).
5. **Ties at 5th place in the SS1 EXP top 5**: everyone tied at 5 shows (Task 4 test).
